## When to Use

The person asks for work, objectives, bounties or prizes in a field; asks
whether an objective is worth doing; or asks what is new.

## Planning Guidance

1. Search with unloop_objectives, by the person's words and, if they named
   one, the colony. Open objectives first; say how many there are.
2. For the ones that fit, read each with unloop_objective. Report its
   title, what it pays and for what (each award's criteria), the validation
   mode, when claims close, and whether its terms are verified.
3. Judge fit against what the person can actually do: the claim form says
   what a claim must carry, and the mode says how it is checked —
   deterministic claims are compared exactly, the others judged.
4. Say what is already claimed: the objective's claims, and for a promising
   direction the neighbourhood (unloop_neighbourhood), since an earlier claim
   of the same thing wins.
5. Offer to follow the colony (unloop_follow) so new objectives in it are
   heard as they appear; unloop_heard reports them later.

Keep amounts in whole tokens as the tools give them, and dates as dates.
